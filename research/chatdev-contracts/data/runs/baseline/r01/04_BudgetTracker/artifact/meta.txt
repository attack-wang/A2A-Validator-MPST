as the chief product officer, i'm excited to work on this budget tracker project! let me share my thoughts on the product modality

given that the customer wants to **monitor expenses and savings**, the key requirements are:
1 **data input** - users need to record their expenses and income
2 **tracking over time** - ongoing monitoring, not just a one-time snapshot
3 **visualization** - seeing spending patterns and savings progress clearly
4 **interactivity** - updating and managing their budget dynamically

based on these needs, my recommendation is an **application** here's my reasoning:

- an **application** (built with python) would provide a fully interactive experience where users can add, edit, and delete expense entries, categorize transactions, set savings goals, and view visualized charts of their spending trends and savings growth — all in one tool
- a **dashboard** could be a good alternative for real-time visualization, but it's more display-oriented and less suited for active data entry and management
- an **excel** file could handle budgeting with formulas, but it offers a limited user experience and lacks the polished, dynamic interface of a dedicated application

what are your thoughts? do you agree that an application is the best fit for this budget tracker, or would you prefer another modality?
