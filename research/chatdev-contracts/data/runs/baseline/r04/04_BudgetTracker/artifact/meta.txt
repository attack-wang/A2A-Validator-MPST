as the chief product officer, i'd like to share my thoughts on the best product modality for this budget tracker task

given the customer's need to **monitor expenses and savings**, the product should ideally support:
1 **data input** – users need to record their expenses and income regularly
2 **dynamic calculation** – tracking totals, category breakdowns, and savings over time
3 **visualization** – charts showing spending trends, budget vs actual, and savings growth

with these requirements in mind, my recommendation is an **application** a python-based application can provide an interactive interface where users can add, edit, and delete expense entries, categorize them, and view real-time visualizations (pie charts for category breakdowns, line charts for savings trends) it offers the best balance of interactivity and functionality for personal finance management

alternatively, a **dashboard** could work well if the customer prioritizes at-a-glance visualization of their financial status, though it may be less suited for hands-on data entry an **excel** file is another lightweight option, but it lacks the polished, interactive experience of a dedicated tool
