Palindrome Playground
=====================
Words to spot: level, radar, civic, kayak, madam, refer, rotor, noon.
Some of these are not: hello, world, python, palindrome.
Famous phrases:
A man, a plan, a canal: Panama!
Madam, in Eden, I'm Adam.
Was it a car or a cat I saw?
No lemon, no melon.
Never odd or even.
Whole lines that mirror themselves:
Was it a car or a cat I saw?
This line is definitely not a palindrome.
Able was I, ere I saw Elba.
Almost there: palindromeemordnilap is a trick one.
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
