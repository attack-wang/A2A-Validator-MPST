as the chief product officer, i've analyzed the customer's request for a budget tracker to monitor expenses and savings let me share my thoughts on the product modality

**my recommendation: application**

here's my reasoning:

1 **interactivity is essential**: a budget tracker fundamentally requires users to *input* data — adding expenses, categorizing them, setting savings goals among our available modalities, only an **application** supports this two-way interaction static formats (image, pdf, document, powerpoint) can only display information, not capture it

2 **functional requirements fit well**: a python-based application can implement:
   - expense entry with categories (food, transport, entertainment, etc)
   - savings tracking with progress toward goals
   - visualized summaries (pie charts for spending breakdown, line charts for trends)
   - data persistence so the tracker remains useful over time

3 **why not other modalities?**
   - **excel** could handle formulas and charts, but it's less user-friendly as a dedicated "tracker" and lacks a polished, purpose-built interface
   - **dashboard** excels at real-time *display*, but a budget tracker needs input functionality beyond passive monitoring
   - **website** is viable, but a standalone application better serves a personal finance tool without deployment dependencies

an application gives the customer a real, usable tool rather than just a presentation of information

what's your perspective on this? do you agree that an application is the best fit, or do you see advantages in another modality?
