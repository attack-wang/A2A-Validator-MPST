Modality:
as the chief product officer, i'd like to share my thoughts on the best product modality for our customer's budget tracker

**my recommendation: application**

here's my reasoning:

1 **interactivity is key**: a budget tracker needs users to actively input expenses, categorize transactions, and update savings goals an application (implemented via python) provides the interactive interface needed for this ongoing data entry

2 **dynamic functionality**: unlike static formats (pdf, document, powerpoint), an application can:
   - allow users to add, edit, and delete expense entries
   - automatically calculate totals and savings
   - generate real-time visualizations (pie charts for spending categories, line charts for savings trends)

3 **user experience**: a budget tracker is a tool people use regularly, not a one-time presentation an application offers the persistent, reusable experience the customer needs

4 **feasibility**: our development team can realize this via python with a gui framework, making it fully implementable within our capabilities

**alternative consideration**: a dashboard could also work for visualizing financial data in real-time, but it's more suited for monitoring pre-existing data streams rather than interactive expense management

what are your thoughts? do you agree with the application modality, or would you prefer another approach?
