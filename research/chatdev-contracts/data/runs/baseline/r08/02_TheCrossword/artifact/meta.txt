3 validate letters in real-time
4 detect and confirm puzzle completion

**my recommendation: application**

here's my reasoning:
- the crossword puzzle is fundamentally an **interactive tool** — it requires continuous user input, dynamic validation feedback, and state tracking (which cells are filled, which words are complete)
- static formats like image, document, powerpoint, pdf, or excel cannot support the interactive validation and completion detection the customer explicitly requested
- a python-based application can elegantly handle the game loop: rendering the grid, prompting for clue number/direction input, checking letter matches against the solution, and announcing victory when all words are correctly placed
- while a website (html/javascript) could also provide interactivity, the application modality gives us more robust control over game logic and validation, and aligns well with our previous experience building visualized games and tools
