as the chief product officer, i'd like to share my thoughts on the best product modality for this budget tracker task

given the customer's need to "monitor expenses and savings," the key requirements are: **interactivity** (users need to input and update their expenses), **visualization** (tracking trends over time), and **functionality** (it should act as a working tool, not just a static presentation)

my recommendation is **application** here's my reasoning:

1 **interactivity**: a python-based application allows users to add, edit, and delete expense entries, categorize spending, and set savings goals — something static formats like pdf, powerpoint, or image cannot achieve
2 **visualization**: the application can integrate charts (pie charts for expense breakdown, line charts for savings growth) directly within the tool
3 **realizability**: we have proven capability in delivering python applications, and a budget tracker fits perfectly as a software tool
4 **user experience**: a tracker implies ongoing usage, which demands a dynamic interface rather than a one-time document

a **dashboard** could also be a viable alternative since it excels at real-time monitoring, but it's typically better suited for displaying pre-existing data streams rather than serving as a personal input-driven tracking tool

what are your thoughts? do you agree with application, or would you prefer another modality such as dashboard or excel?
