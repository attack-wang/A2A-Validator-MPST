as the chief product officer, i've analyzed the customer's requirements for this task the key demands are:

1 **interactivity**: players must click to uncover cells and flag suspected mines
2 **real-time updates**: the board must refresh with updated markings after every move
3 **visual distinction**: flagged cells need a distinct visual representation
4 **game logic**: mine placement, adjacency counting, win/lose conditions across 3 difficulty levels

given these requirements, the product must be **interactive and dynamic** — static formats like image, document, powerpoint, or pdf cannot support click-based gameplay or real-time board updates

in my opinion, **application** is the ideal modality for this task here's why:

- a python-based application (eg, using pygame or tkinter) fully supports mouse-driven interactions: left-click to uncover, right-click to flag
- it can render the grid-based board with distinct visuals for covered cells, uncovered numbers, mines, and flags
- game state (3 difficulty levels: beginner 9×9/10 mines, intermediate 16×16/40 mines, expert 30×16/99 mines) can be managed seamlessly in real-time
- chatdev has proven experience delivering visualized games as python applications, which reduces development risk

a website (html) could also technically work, but for a classic desktop-style game like minesweeper, a standalone application offers better performance, cleaner game-loop control, and a more polished player experience

do you agree that we should proceed with **application** as our product modality?
