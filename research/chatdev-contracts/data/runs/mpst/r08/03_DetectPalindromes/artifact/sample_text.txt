Palindrome Finder - Sample Text
===============================
A man, a plan, a canal: Panama!
Was it a car or a cat I saw?
Never odd or even.
Madam, in Eden, I'm Adam.
Here are some palindromic words: level, radar, civic, noon, madam,
rotor, kayak, refer, stats, tenet, deed, eye, racecar.
Some ordinary words follow: hello, world, python, programming,
detection, software, example, sample, text, file.
Did you notice that "deified" is also a palindrome? So is "reviver"
and "repaper". The word "palindrome" itself is not a palindrome,
which is one of language's little ironies.
Numbers can be palindromic too: 12321, 4554, 7, 1001.
This line contains no palindromes at all, probably.
