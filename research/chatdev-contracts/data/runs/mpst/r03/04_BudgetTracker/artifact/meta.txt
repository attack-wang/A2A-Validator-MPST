as the chief product officer, i'd like to share my analysis on the product modality for this budget tracker task

**understanding the customer's needs:**
the customer wants a "budget tracker" to monitor expenses and savings the keyword here is "tracker" — this implies:
1 **interactivity**: users need to input/record expenses over time
2 **data management**: categorization, summation, and persistence of financial records
3 **monitoring**: ongoing visualization of spending patterns and savings progress

**evaluating the modalities:**

- **excel**: suitable for manual budget sheets, but lacks an interactive experience; users must manage formulas and formatting themselves, which adds friction
- **dashboard**: excellent for visualizing real-time information, but it's primarily display-oriented — weaker on data entry and record-keeping
- **website**: possible, but a static html file limits persistent data storage and dynamic interaction
- **application**: a python-based application can combine everything the customer needs — an interactive interface for logging expenses, automatic calculations of totals and savings, category breakdowns, and visual charts (pie/bar charts) for monitoring trends it's fully realizable and delivers a complete "tool" experience

**my recommendation:**
i strongly recommend **application** a budget tracker is fundamentally a personal finance *tool*, not just a static presentation of information an application gives the customer an all-in-one solution: input, tracking, calculation, and visualization in a single interactive product
