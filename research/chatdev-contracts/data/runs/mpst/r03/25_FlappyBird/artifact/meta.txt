   - straightforward collision detection between the bird, pipes, and ground
   - a self-contained deliverable the customer can run locally without dependencies on a browser environment

3 **realizability**: a python-based game application is fully within our development capability, and it maps cleanly onto every requirement the customer listed — flap mechanic, random pipe gaps, scoring, game-over conditions, and gradual difficulty adjustment
