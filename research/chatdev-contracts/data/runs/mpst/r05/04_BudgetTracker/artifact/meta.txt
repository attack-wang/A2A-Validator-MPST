as the chief product officer, i'd like to share my thoughts on the product modality for our customer's budget tracker

**my analysis of the requirements:**
the customer wants to *monitor* expenses and savings, which implies:
1 **data entry capability** – users need to record expenses and savings over time
2 **dynamic tracking** – the data accumulates and updates continuously
3 **visualization** – seeing spending patterns, category breakdowns, and savings progress

**my recommendation: application**

here's my reasoning:
- an **application** (via python) can provide an interactive tool where users can add, edit, and delete expense entries, categorize spending, set savings goals, and view charts — all in one place it fully satisfies the "tracker" nature of the request
- a **dashboard** is a strong alternative since it excels at real-time visualization, but it's more display-oriented and weaker on interactive data entry
- **excel** could technically track budgets, but it's static and places the burden of formulas and maintenance on the user — not a polished product experience
- modalities like document, pdf, powerpoint, image, or mind map are presentation-oriented and cannot support ongoing tracking functionality

given the customer's need for an ongoing, interactive monitoring tool, i believe **application** is the most realizable and user-friendly choice

what's your opinion? do you agree with application, or would you prefer dashboard or another modality?
