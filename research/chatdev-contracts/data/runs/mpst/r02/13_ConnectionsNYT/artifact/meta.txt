Task:
Develop a puzzle game where the player must group 16 words into four sets of four, based on hidden categories. The words are presented in a 4x4 grid, and players select four at a time to form a group. Correct groups are removed and revealed with a category and a color-coded difficulty (yellow, green, blue, purple). Incorrect guesses count as mistakes, with a maximum of four allowed. Only one correct solution exists, though words may appear to fit multiple categories. Include shuffle functionality and provide immediate feedback after each guess. A new puzzle is generated daily.

as the chief product officer, i've analyzed the customer's requirements for this word-grouping puzzle game let me share my thoughts on the most suitable product modality:

**my recommendation: website (html)**

here's my reasoning:

1 **interactivity is core to this product**: the game requires click-based word selection, drag-free grouping, shuffle functionality, immediate visual feedback (color-coded reveals, mistake counters), and dynamic grid updates a website built with html/css/javascript handles all of these interactions natively and smoothly

2 **daily puzzle accessibility**: since a new puzzle is generated daily, a website is ideal — players can simply open the page in any browser without installing anything the daily generation logic can be implemented via javascript based on the date seed

3 **visual design requirements**: the color-coded difficulty system (yellow, green, blue, purple) and category reveal animations are straightforward to implement with css, giving us full control over the polished look and feel

4 **shareability**: puzzle games thrive on players sharing their results (like the famous daily word games) a website makes this frictionless

5 **cross-platform**: works on desktop and mobile browsers alike, maximizing our potential player base

the alternative would be an application via python, but that would require users to have python installed, which adds friction for a casual daily puzzle game and limits shareability

i believe **website** is the clear winner here do you agree with this choice?
