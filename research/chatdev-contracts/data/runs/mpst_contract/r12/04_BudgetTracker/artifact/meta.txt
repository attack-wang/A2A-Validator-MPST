as the chief product officer, i'd like to share my thoughts on the best product modality for our customer's budget tracker

given the task is to "create a budget tracker to monitor expenses and savings," the key requirements are:
1 **interactivity** - users need to input/log their expenses and savings over time
2 **visualization** - users need to see where their money goes (charts, breakdowns)
3 **dynamic updates** - the tracker should reflect new data as it's added

considering these needs, i believe an **application** would be the ideal choice a python-based application can provide:
- interactive input forms for logging expenses and savings
- category-based expense tracking (food, transport, entertainment, etc)
- visual charts (pie charts for expense breakdown, line charts for savings growth)
- persistent data storage so users can update their budget over time
- summary reports of spending habits

a **dashboard** could also be a strong candidate since it excels at real-time visualization, but it's typically more display-oriented, whereas an application offers both data entry and visualization capabilities that a budget tracker fundamentally requires

excel could work as a simpler alternative, but it lacks the polished, interactive user experience of a dedicated application

what are your thoughts? do you agree that an application is the best fit, or would you prefer another modality?
